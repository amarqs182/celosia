# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are a small group of hobbyists building a static recompilation of *Shadow of the Colossus*.
We want working on this project to be straightforward and enjoyable for everyone, regardless of
experience level, background, identity, or how much time they can contribute.

We pledge to keep this a technical, welcoming space where people can ask basic questions,
disagree about design, and learn in public without being judged for it.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that keeps this project healthy:

- Be respectful. Critique code, not people. Explain *why* something is a problem.
- Give constructive reviews: point at the specific line, suggest a concrete alternative, and
  distinguish blocking issues from personal preference.
- Assume good intent. Most disagreements come from missing context, not malice.
- Keep discussions technical and on topic. Move long design debates into an issue.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change; a rejected patch is not a rejection of you.
- Credit other people's work, including tooling, documentation, and reverse-engineering research.
- Remember that this project depends on reverse-engineering tooling and the community around it.
  Contributors from that community, and the tools they publish, must be treated respectfully.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Discriminatory comments or imagery related to identity, background, or experience.
- Sexualized language or attention, and unwelcome contact of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission.
- Encouraging or coordinating any of the above.

## Enforcement

Maintainers review every report and decide on a response proportionate to the behavior and its
history. Possible consequences, applied progressively:

1. **Warning** — a private note explaining what was wrong and what is expected going forward.
2. **Temporary ban** — a time-limited removal from discussions and pull requests.
3. **Permanent ban** — for severe incidents or repeated violations after prior warnings.

Maintainers who are themselves involved in an incident will not take part in deciding its outcome.

## Reporting

Report problems by opening a private GitHub Security Advisory on this repository, or by contacting
a maintainer directly through GitHub. Do not open a public issue about a conduct incident.

Reports are kept confidential: only the maintainers handling the case will see them, and details
are shared no further than needed to resolve it. Retaliation against anyone who reports an issue
in good faith is itself a violation of this code.
